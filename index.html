How it works

  1. 01

    A mockup

    I make a mockup of your website to show you some ideas I have. If you like, you can give me your input before I even start.

  2. 02

    Your custom site

    You give me the details I need, approve the design, and tell me what to change. We keep going until it's the final product.

  3. 03

    Handoff

    I hand over all your site files. Together we set up:

    • A GitHub account to store your site files.
    • A Cloudflare account to host your site.

    Both accounts are in your name, so you own your site. You give me access so I can get it all set up and working for you.

  4. 04

    Managing your site

    You take it from here

    Once I've fully set up your site, you remove my access to your GitHub and Cloudflare. Your live site is yours and good to go. From then on, you're in charge of changes.

    I manage your site

    For a monthly fee I stay added and make any changes you need. Contact me any time. I'll also make you a client account on my site where you can request changes and keep track of your plan.

Every project is priced on its own. A simple site costs less than one with more pages or features like bookings. Web apps and mobile apps cost more. Tell me what you need and I'll give you a price.

Websites

Website · The one you are on

haydnmcintyre.ca

This site, designed and built to replace the first one. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript again, with no framework and no build step, and three.js behind it for the one thing that needed it: the whole site is a single push forward through a starfield, with the brass HM mark hanging ahead of you in the hero and the camera flying straight through it as you scroll, and every section arrives from the vanishing point. The previews browse each project for you, scrolling it and clicking through it the way a visitor would. Both pages are one document, the contact form posts to a serverless endpoint that mails the message through and falls back to the visitor's own mail app, and structured data and a share card sit behind the page for search and social. The scene waits until the browser is idle and stays away on a slow connection, so the page reads first. Hosting, domain and certificate set up alongside, as before.
